Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the Borcama iOS app. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

1. Data controller

The data controller is the Borcama service, operated by Özer Öcek (sole proprietor), Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: zero@borcama.com. For any data protection request, write to the same address.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, account ID, sign-up and last sign-in times. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the email address (or Apple private relay address) and identifier Apple shares with us.
  • Financial records you enter: bank and card names, debts, credit limits, statements, payments, income and spending. If you import a statement, the file is read to extract these records.
  • Subscription data: whether you have Borcama Pro, the product, purchase and renewal status, and an anonymous purchase identifier provided via Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your card or payment details.
  • Technical and security data: session, device and app version information and limited logs needed to prevent abuse.
  • Support communications: messages you send us.

Borcama never asks for your online banking password, full card number, CVV, national ID number or biometric data. Face ID / Touch ID lock is handled by your device; we never receive biometric data. Please do not type such information into free-text fields.

3. No tracking or advertising in the iOS app

The iOS app does not load Google Analytics, Google Ads or any other advertising or cross-app tracking tools, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not show ads. We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Purposes and legal bases (GDPR Art. 6)

  • Creating your account, secure sign-in, and providing debt, payment, income and spending tracking, statement comparison, interest estimates and debt plans — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Providing and verifying Borcama Pro — performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security, abuse prevention, fixing errors and improving the service — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)), which we balance against your rights.
  • Answering support requests — performance of a contract or legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)).
  • Complying with legal obligations (e.g. responding to lawful requests from authorities) — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Optional features or notifications that require it — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time without affecting processing before withdrawal.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Service providers (processors)

We share data only as needed to run the service, under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase — authentication and database.
  • Vercel — hosting.
  • Cloudflare — security checks.
  • Sentry — technical error monitoring (see section 7).
  • Apple — App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat — managing and verifying in-app subscriptions.
  • Google (Gemini API) — only if you use the in-app assistant: your question and a summary of the relevant records you entered are sent to generate an answer.
  • Resend — sending account and service emails.

We may also disclose data to competent public authorities where required by law.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred outside the EEA, UK or Türkiye, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), and, for Türkiye, the transfer mechanisms of KVKK Article 9. You can request a copy of the relevant safeguards by contacting us.

7. Error reports

Sentry error reports contain the app version, error type and code location. Statement and form contents, financial records, email addresses and the private contents of error messages are not sent. Screen recordings and click histories are not collected. The provider can technically see your IP address while connecting; the user IP field in reports is masked.

8. Retention

  • Account data and the financial records you enter are kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Security and legal records are kept only for the limited period needed for their purpose: security logs for up to 12 months, and records we must keep by law (such as tax records) for the period the law requires.
  • Support messages are kept for up to 24 months after your request is resolved.

When a retention period ends, data is deleted, destroyed or anonymized. Purchase records held by Apple are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

9. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated Borcama data at any time in the app under Settings → “Delete my account”, or by emailing zero@borcama.com. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage that in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

10. Security

We use access controls, row-level authorization, encrypted (TLS) connections and abuse-prevention measures. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and authorities of a personal data breach where required by law.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and KVKK Article 11), you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and receive a copy,
  • rectify inaccurate or incomplete data,
  • erasure of your data,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests,
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent,
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

Send requests to zero@borcama.com with information that lets us verify your identity. We respond within one month, which may be extended where the law allows.

12. Children

Borcama is not directed to children and is intended for adults managing their own finances. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

When this policy is updated, the new version and effective date are published on this page. If a change materially affects the purposes or legal bases of processing, we will also notify you in the app or by email.

Privacy questions: zero@borcama.com
