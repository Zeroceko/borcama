Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for Borcama Pro monthly and yearly subscriptions purchased in the iOS app. Borcama Pro is a digital subscription. For purchases made in the iOS app, Apple is the seller through the App Store.

1. Renewal

Borcama Pro renews automatically at the end of the monthly or yearly period you chose. The renewal price and frequency are shown on the purchase screen. Unless you cancel, your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or via the “Manage subscription in the App Store” link in the app. To stop the next renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period: you are not charged again and keep Pro features until then.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically refund any amount already charged.

3. Refunds are handled by Apple

Because Apple processes all App Store payments, Borcama cannot issue refunds for App Store purchases. Refund requests are decided by Apple under its own policies. To request a refund:

  • Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase.
  • Or use the “Report a Problem” link in the purchase receipt Apple emailed you.
  • If you need help, contact us at zero@borcama.com with your purchase email and, if possible, the receipt number. We can help you reach Apple's refund process and resolve access problems.

When Apple approves a refund, it is sent to the original payment method under Apple's processes; the time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer. Borcama Pro access may end once the refund is completed.

4. Technical problems

If a persistent technical problem prevents you from using the Pro features you paid for, please contact Borcama support first. If we cannot fix it, you can request a refund from Apple as described above.

5. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit any consumer rights that cannot be waived under the law of your country of residence. Where Apple's terms in force at the time of the transaction or mandatory law provide greater protection, that protection applies. Apple's current terms are available here.
