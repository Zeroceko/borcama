## English (U.S.) — ayrıca English (U.K.) için aynı metin

- **Subtitle:** `Debt payoff & budget planner`
- **Promotional text:** `See every card, loan and bill in one place. Know what to pay this month, which debt to clear first and what is left over.`
- **Keywords:** `debt,payoff,budget,credit card,loan,bills,expense,tracker,payments,snowball,avalanche,money,planner`

**Description**

```
Borcama turns your debt into a plan.

Add your credit cards, loans and overdrafts, mark your payments, and get three answers every month:

• What do I pay? Every amount and due date in one list.
• Which debt first? A payoff order based on your own balances and interest rates — avalanche or snowball.
• What is left? Income, spending and fixed payments in one monthly picture.

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Use every feature right away. An account is only needed if you want to sync between devices and back up your data.

NO BANK LOGIN
Borcama never asks for your bank password. You enter what matters, in your own currency.

PAYMENT REMINDERS
Optional reminders the day before a payment. The lock screen never shows amounts or bank names.

FACE ID / TOUCH ID LOCK
Keep your finances behind your device biometrics.

YOUR LANGUAGE, YOUR CURRENCY
English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano and Türkçe. USD, EUR, GBP and more.

Borcama does not lend money, broker loans or give financial advice. Calculations are based on the information you enter.

Terms of Use: https://borcama.com/terms
Privacy Policy: https://borcama.com/privacy
```

**What's New**

```
Borcama now speaks English, German, French, Spanish and Italian, and works in your own currency. Pick your language and currency on the welcome screen.
```

## App Review Notes (İngilizce ek paragraf)

```
1.69.0 LOCALIZATION AND REGIONS: The app follows the device language (Turkish, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian) and can be changed on the welcome screen and in Settings > Language. On first launch the currency follows the device region and can be changed. Outside Türkiye, Borcama Pro is not offered: when the App Store has no subscription products for the storefront, all Pro purchase screens are hidden and Pro features are available for free. Statement import supports Turkish banks only and is shown only when the currency is TRY. All features remain usable without an account ("Continue without an account"). In-app purchases use StoreKit only; there are no referral or promo codes, email-link sign-in or external payment links in the app.
```
